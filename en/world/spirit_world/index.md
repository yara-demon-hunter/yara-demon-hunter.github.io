## Beyond the spiritual eye

Not every place in this world can be walked to. In [Yara, Demon Hunter: Lost in the Mist - Mini RPG]({{ '/en/mini-games/yara-rpg/' | relative_url }}), a hidden realm emerges between the mist and the stars for those able to see beyond the veil.

## Presences in the mist

Spirits cross into the living world as well. Travelers in the game meet river spirits, specters and stranger figures who offer bargains, and the mist always asks for something in return.
