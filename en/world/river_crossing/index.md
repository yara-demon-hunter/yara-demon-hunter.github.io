## Water through the woods

A stream runs down through the forest toward the village, and fishermen work its banks alongside the woodcutters. On the day the bell failed, they were among the first to fall silent.

## A saying about rivers

The people of the valley carry the river in their words too. Zeph remembers his mother saying that names are like currents, and that we choose where the lives of those we love will flow.

## In the mini RPG

In [Yara, Demon Hunter: Lost in the Mist - Mini RPG]({{ '/en/mini-games/yara-rpg/' | relative_url }}), crossing the river is the second stage of the journey. River demons, golems, shadow creatures and vampires guard the far side, where the mist still hides danger.
