## A forest that feeds the village

Woodcutters earn the village's living here, and as the settlement grows they must go deeper among the trees. Wagons carry the logs home along the forest path, while the stream gives the fishermen their work.

## When the mist comes

The danger does not always announce itself. The sky darkens, the birds go quiet, and a thin white mist slides between the roots before rising around the men's boots. Within moments, no one can see the way back.

## The laughter

Those who escaped the mist speak of laughter from every direction, of horses panicking and of silhouettes watching between the trees. It was here that Zeph disappeared on the day the bell rang too late.

## In the mini RPG

The Misty Forest is the first region of [Yara, Demon Hunter: Lost in the Mist - Mini RPG]({{ '/en/mini-games/yara-rpg/' | relative_url }}), where shadow wolves, witches, skeletons and corrupted specters wait in the fog.
