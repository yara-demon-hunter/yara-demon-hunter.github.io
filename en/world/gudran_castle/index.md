## The heart of the village

The settlement grew around Lord Gudran's small castle. Its gates open onto the square, and its orders reach every house, including the one that matters most: after the bell, no one enters the woods.

## The lord at the gates

When the village gathered to demand a rescue for Zeph, Lord Gudran came out past the gates with his long silver beard and his staff, flanked by soldiers in heavy armor. His grey eyes swept the square, and everyone there felt the weight of his authority.

## In the mini RPG

[Yara, Demon Hunter: Lost in the Mist - Mini RPG]({{ '/en/mini-games/yara-rpg/' | relative_url }}) imagines a darker road to these walls. Ancient creatures and knights guard the castle, and beyond its doors, in the Throne Chamber, Gudran waits at the heart of his domain.
