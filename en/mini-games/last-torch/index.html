How a night works

  1. Hunt Kill monsters for XP, gold and the hour's quota.
  2. Finish them Quota met? Clear the screen to end the hour.
  3. Camp Buy relics, feed the torch or rest.
  4. Survive Bosses wait at hours 4, 8 and 12. Then, dawn.

You lose if the hunter or the torch dies, so you can never just run away.

Skills, relics and omens

Blade

Melee power, Spectral Blades and Corpse Burst.

Shadow

Speed, daggers, Fan of Knives and Shadow Clone.

Flame

Fury, health and a torch that fights back.

Curse

XP, gold, Storm Sigil and Starfall.

The skill tree resets every night. Maxed powers can evolve, like Spectral Blades into Blade Tempest or Flame Sentinel into Sun Spire. At camp, gold buys 22 relics, and from Hour 3 omens such as Blood Moon trade extra danger for extra rewards.

Four hunters to unlock

Winning opens the endless night and eight Darkness levels. Your trophies, feats and bestiary stay in the Sanctuary.

Controls

Computer

Move with the mouse or WASD. Left click throws daggers. Space, Shift or right click dashes.

Phone

Hold it sideways. Joystick to move. Hold the dagger button to aim and release to throw.
