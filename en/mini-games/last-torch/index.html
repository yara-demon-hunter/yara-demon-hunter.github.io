How a night works

  1. Hunt Kill monsters for XP, gold and the hour's quota.
  2. Finish them Quota met? Clear the screen to end the hour.
  3. Camp Buy relics, feed the torch or rest.
  4. Survive Bosses wait at hours 4, 8 and 12. Then, dawn.

You lose if the hunter or the torch dies, so you can never just run away.

Four ways to play

Blade

The warrior. Charge in and kill everything: Bloodlust, Earthbreaker, War Cry and Executioner.

Shadow

The thief. Cheap but technical: mark with daggers, detonate with the dash, hide in smoke where every hit crits.

Flame

The keeper. Calm and defensive: a ring of fire, a Vigil shield and a torch that can raise you back.

Curse

The sorcerer. Spells from afar: Grave Wisp, Creeping Hex and a Void Rift that drags the horde in.

The skill tree resets every night. Maxed powers can evolve, like Earthbreaker into Cataclysm or Void Rift into Event Horizon. At camp, gold buys 27 relics, and from Hour 3 omens such as Blood Moon trade extra danger for extra rewards.

Swear an oath

At the end of each branch waits an Oath, shown here with the skills of its branch. You can swear only one per night: it gives a powerful bonus, costs you something and changes how the hunter looks. Learning the two skills that lead to it opens the oath.

Four hunters to unlock

Winning opens the endless night and eight Darkness levels. Your trophies, feats and bestiary stay in the Sanctuary.

Controls

Computer

Move with the mouse or WASD. Left click throws daggers (hold to charge a Charged Dagger Throw). Space, Shift or right click dashes.

Phone

Hold it upright or sideways. Joystick to move. Tap the dagger button to throw at the nearest monster, or drag it to aim and release to throw.
