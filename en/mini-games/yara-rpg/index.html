How it plays

Explore

Find gold, chests, events and monsters in each region.

Fight

Pick your moves by turn. Strike during a warning to interrupt the enemy.

Equip

Fill ten slots with gear and bring a companion to battle.

Choose

Every moral choice pulls your soul toward light or shadow.

Spirits may approach you in the mist with offers, and what you earn in the Sanctuary survives even death.
