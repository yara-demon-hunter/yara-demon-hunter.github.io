## In the story

Zeph works the forest with his friend Oskar, and since Amara told him the news, going home means something new. He respects the rule of the bell, and his father-in-law Darian's warnings about the woods stay with him.

## The day of the mist

When the bell failed to ring on time, the mist closed in and Zeph was cut off from the others. He later woke in a dark hut, deep in the forest, still thinking of Amara and of the child he has not met.

## In the mini RPG

Zeph is one of the first characters you can choose in [Yara, Demon Hunter: Lost in the Mist - Mini RPG]({{ '/en/mini-games/yara-rpg/' | relative_url }}): a watchful survivor who starts with extra Focus.
