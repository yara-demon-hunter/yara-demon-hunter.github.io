## In Last Torch

The Demon Hunter guards the last flame through a twelve-hour night in [Last Torch]({{ '/en/mini-games/last-torch/' | relative_url }}), fighting with an automatic blade, thrown daggers, a dash and the burst of Fury.

## In the mini RPG

In [Yara, Demon Hunter: Lost in the Mist - Mini RPG]({{ '/en/mini-games/yara-rpg/' | relative_url }}), the Demon Hunter is a secret character, unlocked by defeating Gudran. A full set of matching gear also waits to be found: the sword, the mask and the cloak.
