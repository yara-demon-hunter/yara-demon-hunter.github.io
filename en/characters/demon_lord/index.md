## In Last Torch

In [Last Torch]({{ '/en/mini-games/last-torch/' | relative_url }}), the Demon Lord arrives at Hour 4 with bursts and rings of fire, marked slams on the ground and swarms of bats. Slaying him unlocks the Ash Reaver.

## In the mini RPG

In [Yara, Demon Hunter: Lost in the Mist - Mini RPG]({{ '/en/mini-games/yara-rpg/' | relative_url }}), he is one of the strongest special monsters of the journey. "All demons bow before me."
