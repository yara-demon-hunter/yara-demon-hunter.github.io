## In the story

Oskar teases, jokes and works beside Zeph every day. When the mist fell, he tried to go after his friend and had to be dragged onto a wagon by the other men. He promised himself he would come back.

## Back in the village

Oskar brings the news to Amara and Darian, then tries to return to the forest with commander Cássio, even after the bell. That choice puts him directly against Captain Brutos and Lord Gudran's order.

## In the mini RPG

In [Yara, Demon Hunter: Lost in the Mist - Mini RPG]({{ '/en/mini-games/yara-rpg/' | relative_url }}), Oskar is a relentless fighter who prefers to end a fight with one powerful blow, with a bonus to his total Attack.
