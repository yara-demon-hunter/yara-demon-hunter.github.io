## In the story

When Oskar and commander Cássio prepare to enter the forest after the bell, Captain Brutos blocks their way. Amara begs him to let them go, but he holds to the order: after the bell, no one leaves the village.

## In the mini RPG

In [Yara, Demon Hunter: Lost in the Mist - Mini RPG]({{ '/en/mini-games/yara-rpg/' | relative_url }}), Brutos is a special monster who bars the road to the heart of Gudran Castle. You can face him or retreat. "Brutos smash!"
