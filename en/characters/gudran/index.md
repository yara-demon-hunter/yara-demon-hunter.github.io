## In the story

The village carries Lord Gudran's name and grew around his small castle. His order is clear: once the bell sounds, no one enters the woods. When the square filled with people demanding a rescue, he came out past the gates with his staff and his long silver beard, and everyone knelt.

## In the mini RPG

In [Yara, Demon Hunter: Lost in the Mist - Mini RPG]({{ '/en/mini-games/yara-rpg/' | relative_url }}), Gudran waits in the Throne Chamber at the end of the journey. "This kingdom will always be mine." Defeating him also unlocks new characters.
