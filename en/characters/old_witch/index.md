## In the mini RPG

Among the figures met in the mist of [Yara, Demon Hunter: Lost in the Mist - Mini RPG]({{ '/en/mini-games/yara-rpg/' | relative_url }}), few are as tempting as the Old Witch. An old witch offers a dark drink: “Drink it all. It will drain your strength, but I will pay you well.” Accepting pays 150 gold, but leaves you with no more than 20% of your health.
