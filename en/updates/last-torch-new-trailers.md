The Last Torch page has new trailers. Both are 20 seconds long and were recorded from the game itself, in real time and without any speed-up, so what you see is how a night really plays.

## What is in the trailer

Every cut comes from a different part of the night, with different heroes and different powers:

- **Demon Hunter, wave 5.** Surrounded by a swarm, with Hearth Ring, Flaming Sword and Ember Steps lit, and his Fury bursting out.
- **Azael, wave 3.** His new Fury, Blood Waltz, slicing through the crowd with red cuts, next to his Shadow Clone and Shadow Raven.
- **A dialogue before the boss.** Azael taunts the hunter before the Demon Lord arrives.
- **The Demon Lord fight.** Curse powers at work: Storm Sigil, Starfall, Void Rift, Spectral Blades and Soul Nova.
- **Captain Brutos, wave 7.** The Captain's Tremor shaking the screen as he stomps through a horde, with Earthbreaker and War Cry.
- **Another dialogue.** Azael calls Captain Brutos: "This hunter is yours."
- **The finale,** with Phoenix Pact, Fireballs, Flame Sentinel and the title card.

## Two formats

There is a widescreen version (1920×1080) for computers and a vertical one (1080×1620) for phones. The Last Torch page picks the one that fits your screen and plays it automatically.

The trailers show only a taste of what a full night holds. Twelve hours, three bosses and four ways to play are waiting in the game, and you can read about all of them in the game guide.
