Fury is the special that fires by itself when its bar fills. Until now every hero had the same one. From now on each hero has a Fury that fits the way they fight. The bar, the way it charges and the Fury skill in the tree work as before; what changes is the special itself.

## Demon Hunter: Blade Storm

The Demon Hunter keeps the burst of blades in every direction. It is the simplest Fury and a good one for a first night.

<figure><img src="{{ art }}fury-blade-storm.webp" alt="Demon Hunter's Blade Storm Fury sending blades in every direction" width="1280" height="720" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Demon Hunter's Blade Storm.</figcaption></figure>

## Azael: Blood Waltz

Azael vanishes and dashes through the monsters, cutting as he goes.

- Up to **6 cuts**. Each cut jumps to the nearest monster that is *not* the one he just hit, passes through it and leaves a red slash behind.
- With only two monsters around, he goes back and forth between them three times.
- Every cut makes a blade-through-the-air sound and heals him for **30% of the damage** it deals, on top of his Blood Drinker life steal.
- He cannot be hurt while he is cutting.

It is the perfect answer when Azael is low on health and surrounded.

## Captain Brutos: Captain's Tremor

Brutos is slow and heavy, so his Fury is built around his weight. He does not grow and has no final blow. Instead, for the whole Fury, **every step he takes is a stomp**.

- The Fury lasts about **6 seconds**, 2.5 seconds longer than the others.
- Each stomp hurts, pushes and briefly stuns the monsters around him, and the screen shakes.
- He takes **30% less damage** while it lasts.

<figure><img src="{{ art }}fury-captains-tremor.webp" alt="Captain Brutos stomping during his Captain's Tremor Fury, with the screen shaking" width="1280" height="720" loading="lazy"><figcaption>The Captain's Tremor in action.</figcaption></figure>

## Shadow Clone

The shadows of the Shadow Clone skill no longer repeat your Fury. They keep fighting at your side, but the special belongs to the hero alone. With a different Fury per hero, copying it would have turned the screen into a mess and multiplied the damage.

Pick a hero on the start screen and see which Fury suits you.
