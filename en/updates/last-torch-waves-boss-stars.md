This week we changed the pace of every wave and gave players a reason to try again: a **star results screen** at the end of each wave, with records per wave.

## Longer waves, more monsters

- Every wave lasts **at least 2 minutes 24 seconds** and monsters spawn faster and faster until the end.
- When the **boss** arrives, **no regular monsters spawn anymore**: the fight is just you and the boss (monsters the boss summons itself still appear).
- Bosses have **more health**, so the fight has time to show their attacks.
- When the boss falls, **every monster left explodes** and the wave ends right away, with the usual time to grab chests and items.

## Wave results

When you reach camp, the game tallies everything on a results screen, with the coin "tick-tick" sound: **kills, best streak (Rush), gold picked up, damage taken by the torch and by the hero**. Each number turns into points and the total is the **wave score**.


### The 5 stars

| Star | Goal |
|---|---|
| ★ | the **torch** took at most 50% damage |
| ★ | the **torch** took at most 20% damage |
| ★ | the **hero** took at most 50% damage |
| ★ | the **hero** took at most 20% damage |
| ★ | a **streak (Rush)** of 20 + 4 × the wave, or more |

Taking more than 20% damage on the torch or the hero ends the dream of 5 stars. Stars give **more gold**, and all 5 also give **+1 skill point**. Each wave keeps its best result: beat the record and you see **NEW BEST**.

## Counted embers and night records

When the night ends, the Altar's **Embers** appear counting line by line: night, waves, kills, bosses and now also **stars**. If you beat your record for wave, kills, stars or Embers, a **NEW BEST** badge shows up. The death screen and the results stay on screen longer, so there is time to read them.

## Sanctuary: the stars of each wave

The Sanctuary's Feats tab now has a grid with the **12 waves** and the best stars and score of each. The 5-star ones glow. Two new trophies arrived too: **Starlit** (5 stars on 3 different waves) and **Constellation** (5 stars on all 12 waves), plus **Thousand Souls** (a thousand monsters slain) and a total-kills counter on the end screen.

## Other tweaks

- **Fury** now fills **only from sword hits**. Magic, daggers, items and kills from other sources no longer fill the bar.
- The hero's **dashed red circle** is thicker and stronger, so it is easier to see on a phone.
