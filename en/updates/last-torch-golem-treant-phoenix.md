This week Last Torch got new creatures and a lot of fire. Everything was built in 3D in our lab and baked into light sprites, so it runs well even on phones.

## Ember and Moss Golem (from wave 4)

A giant of stone, moss and embers. It is **very slow**, but tough, and has two attacks:

- **Rock throw:** at mid range it raises **one hand** and throws a rock.
- **Ground slam:** up close it raises **both hands** for a moment. A **red circle** on the ground shows where it will land: step out of it, or dash, and you take nothing.

When it falls, it crumbles into rocks and lava shards.


## A wooden Treant

The Treant was rebuilt in 3D, now made of **real-looking wood** with a friendly face (no jump scares). It walks slowly and punches in an arc. The old version stays in the Atelier, just for comparison.

<figure><img src="{{ art }}treant-wood.webp" alt="The wooden Treant walking and punching" width="760" height="323" loading="lazy"><figcaption>The new Treant, side view and punching.</figcaption></figure>

Rowan's **Wicked Oak** also grows more slowly now: at level 1 it takes more than a wave to become the walking oak, and only the **Ancient Grove** evolution turns it into the ancient tree. Leveling the skill speeds everything up.

## Flaming Phoenix (formerly Hearth Phoenix)

- **Flies farther:** it now circles the torch in a much wider loop.
- **Five angles:** you now see it from the side, the front and the back, depending on where it flies.
- **Fire breath:** every 8 seconds it stops throwing fireballs and **breathes a cone of flame** for 1.6 seconds, following the nearest monster. Anything it hits keeps burning.
- Its fireballs are now **less frequent and stronger**, so the screen does not fill up.

## Fire Tornado (replacing Banked Coals)

Every **32 seconds**, if a monster is close, a **tornado of fire** forms over the torch and lasts 5 seconds. It scorches and **pulls toward the center** everything within 190 range, the same maximum reach as the Hearth Ring, and sets monsters ablaze.

<figure><img src="{{ art }}fire-tornado.webp" alt="The Fire Tornado: a funnel of flames spinning over the torch" width="660" height="520" loading="lazy"><figcaption>The fire tornado over the torch.</figcaption></figure>

## Blue Torch: everything turns blue

With the **Blue Torch**, all of the hero's fire turns blue: the sword's flame, the fireballs, the fire on the ground, the flames on burning monsters and even the **Phoenix Pact** bird.
