Until now, holding your phone upright showed a "turn your phone sideways" warning and paused the game. That is gone. **Last Torch now works with the phone held upright or sideways**, whichever you prefer.

## What changed for upright play

- **Same size, taller arena.** The hero and the monsters keep the same size in both positions. Upright, you simply see more of the forest above and below the torch.
- **Controls that fit.** The joystick, the dagger button and the dash button are a bit smaller upright, and the dash sits above the dagger so the two never touch.
- **Messages out of the way.** Tips now appear at the top of the screen so they never cover your thumbs.
- **A skill tree that fits.** The skill tree uses the whole height of the screen: the four paths on top, the tree in the middle and the details below.
- **Dialogues.** In a boss conversation, the two portraits move higher and the speech box spans the whole width at the bottom.
- **Start screen.** The three heroes share the width instead of running off the screen.

## Sideways is still there

Holding the phone sideways keeps the wider view and the same controls you already knew. Both positions are first-class now, so use the one that feels best for the moment, for example upright on the bus and sideways on the couch.
