The night in Last Torch now takes place in the **Misty Forest**, the scenery of the first phase of the [Yara RPG](/en/mini-games/), redrawn to be seen from above.

## What you will see

- **Teal fog and a darker ground,** so the warm light of the torch and the monsters stand out.
- **Glowing mushrooms** that pulse in the dark, with a pale blue light on the ground around them.
- **Ferns and tall grass** that sway as the wind passes, one after the other, like a gust crossing the clearing.
- **Fallen leaves, moss, rocks, roots and stumps** scattered around, and floating blue spores.

## Made to stay out of the way

A fight is what matters, so the scenery was built not to get in the way. Nothing is drawn over the monsters or the hero, and the decorations were thinned out so the ground never competes with what you are fighting. The torch stays the brightest thing on the screen.

## Light enough for a phone

Everything that moves in the forest (ferns, grass, mushrooms) is drawn in just a few frames ahead of time and then only copied onto the screen. That is why the forest can sway and glow without slowing the game down, even on weaker phones. The ground itself is painted once and reused.

The old Dark Forest is still part of the game's library, and new sceneries can be built from the same pieces in the future.
