This week's goal was a game that is **more fun to play and less tiring**: players make real choices, feel they are growing every wave and the screen stays cleaner. Nothing the game already had was replaced; we adjusted prices, pacing and a few details.

## Relics with fixed prices that grow along the tree

It used to be easy to buy the whole shop in a single run. Now each relic has a **fixed price** (it never rises with the wave) and it **grows with its position in the tree**: the first ones in each row cost **300**, and the last ones reach **6,500**. Monsters also drop less gold. With an autopilot test, a run buys 1 to 4 relics per wave and **never the whole shop**: there is always a new choice every wave.


## A pricier Altar

Altar upgrades cost more at every level (the first one still fits a short night, the last ones take dozens of nights), and every **star** earned in the waves gives **1 Ember**. **Extra Dash** left the Altar, and **Swiftness** is now +1% per level (Might, Arcane, Vitality and Fury Power are +2%).

## Active skills cost more points

There is no skill cap, but the skills that act on their own on the field (shoot, explode, summon) now cost **1 extra point per level**, and the strongest evolutions (Sun Spire, Solar Wall, Event Horizon) cost 5. In one night you can carry about 3 or 4 strong powers, and **stacking everything is nearly impossible**. XP is also more gradual: the hero reaches level 8, not 10, on wave 1.

## Less clutter on screen

Skills that used to fire constantly now **fire less often and hit harder**: Flame Sentinel, Sun Spire (it was ~6 bolts per second, now ~2), Grave Wisp, Storm Sigil and the phoenixes' fireballs. Total damage barely changes, but the screen is much cleaner.

## A reorganized Shadow Path

The top row has **Return Fire** and **Shadow Raven**. The second row gathers all the dagger skills: **Ricochet → Death Mark → Dagger Mastery → Fan of Knives**.

## Fireballs can now charge

**Charged Dagger Throw** is gone (the charged dagger pierced everything and made Ricochet useless). Its mechanic moved to **Fireballs**: **hold the dagger button** to charge a **giant fireball**, up to 2.4 times the size and 4 times the damage. The charged ball does not ricochet, and the description warns about it in red.

<figure><img src="{{ art }}fireballs-warning.webp" alt="The Fireballs description with a red warning that the charged ball does not ricochet" width="544" height="660" loading="lazy"><figcaption>The red warning on Fireballs.</figcaption></figure>

## Other balance tweaks

- **Keen Eye** moved to the end of the steel row and is a bit weaker.
- **Holy Water** throws at most 1 vial, and **Blessed Rain** no longer replaces the vials: they keep flying, and the rain falls every 40 seconds.
- **Hearth Ring** and **Event Horizon** lost a bit of strength; **Corpse Burst** and **Soul Nova** gained some.
