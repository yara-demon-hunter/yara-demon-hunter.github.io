<h2 id="what-are-cookies">1. What are cookies?</h2>

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember preferences, understand traffic, and support certain features or advertising tools.

<h2 id="how-we-use-them">2. How we use cookies</h2>

This website may use cookies for several reasons, including:

<ul>
    <li>traffic analysis and website performance measurement;</li>
    <li>remembering language or display preferences;</li>
    <li>supporting the operation of the website and its embedded services;</li>
    <li>advertising and measurement tools, including Google AdSense and related technologies.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="analytics">3. Analytics cookies</h2>

This website may use analytics tools such as Google Analytics to understand visitor behavior, collect technical information, and improve readability, navigation, and content quality.

These tools may record information like pages visited, approximate location, device information, and engagement with the website.

<h2 id="advertising">4. Advertising cookies</h2>

This website may display Google AdSense ads. Advertising systems may use cookies to show relevant ads, measure performance, and prevent repeated display of the same ad.

These systems may also use data from websites and apps that use their services. For more information, see the Google privacy and advertising documentation.

<h2 id="third-party">5. Third-party cookies</h2>

Third-party integrations may place cookies when certain features or services are used. The project does not control the full behavior of third-party cookies, and those services have their own privacy policies and settings.

<h2 id="manage">6. Managing cookies</h2>

You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings. Keep in mind that disabling cookies may affect some features, analytics, or personalization options on the website.

<h2 id="changes">7. Updates to this policy</h2>

This Cookie Policy may be updated as services, tools, or advertising technology change. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect those changes.
