<h2 id="what-is-yara">1. What is Yara, Demon Hunter?</h2>

<strong>Yara, Demon Hunter</strong> is a dark fantasy story and universe created by Miguel Amaral. It includes a web serial, characters, world lore, mini games, and an expanding world designed to grow over time.

<h2 id="is-it-free">2. Is the story free to read?</h2>

Yes. The main story, characters, and most project content are available for free on the website, with the goal of building a reading experience and a community around the universe.

<h2 id="what-kind-of-content">3. What kind of content is on the site?</h2>

The project includes:

<ul>
    <li>book chapters and story content;</li>
    <li>character profiles and lore;</li>
    <li>world locations and atlas entries;</li>
    <li>mini games, such as Last Torch and Yara RPG;</li>
    <li>project updates and public development notes.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="is-the-project-open-source">4. Is the project open source?</h2>

No. The site and the project materials are not opened under a general open-source license. The source code and assets are publicly visible in some contexts, but that does not grant a license to copy, reuse, or redistribute them. Please see the <a href="{{ '/en/copyright/' | relative_url }}">Copyright and Usage</a> page for details.

<h2 id="where-does-the-game-fit">5. Where do the mini games fit into the story?</h2>

The mini games are part of the same universe. They expand the world, show alternate perspectives, and help deepen the lore while keeping the project interactive and exploratory.

<h2 id="will-there-be-more-books">6. Will there be more books or stories?</h2>

Yes. The project is designed to grow over time. New content, chapters, characters, and locations are planned as the universe expands.

<h2 id="can-i-contact-the-project">7. Can I contact the project?</h2>

Yes. Use the <a href="{{ '/en/contact/' | relative_url }}">Contact</a> page or email <a href="mailto:yarademonhunter@gmail.com">yarademonhunter@gmail.com</a> for questions, feedback, partnership proposals, and support.

<h2 id="can-i-support-the-project">8. Can I support the project?</h2>

Yes. The project offers support options through Ko-fi and other channels, and the site also has a dedicated <a href="{{ '/en/support/' | relative_url }}">Support</a> page.

<h2 id="what-is-last-torch">9. What is Last Torch?</h2>

Last Torch is a survival action mini game in which the player fights through a cursed night, unlocks skill upgrades, and survives until dawn. It is designed as a fast, replayable challenge within the same universe.
