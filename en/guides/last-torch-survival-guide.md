    <h1>How to survive your first night in Last Torch</h1>

    <p>Last Torch is not only about keeping the hunter alive. The torch is your second lifeline: if either one falls, the night ends. This guide explains the core decisions that make a first run easier to understand.</p>

    <h2>1. Keep the torch in your plan</h2>
    <p>Watch the torch as closely as your health. Some enemies head straight for the flame, so circling the battlefield without checking on it can cost you the run. Move back toward the torch when pressure builds around it.</p>

    <h2>2. Finish the hour before you relax</h2>
    <p>Each hour has a kill quota. Reaching it stops new monsters from spawning, but the hour does not end until the remaining enemies are defeated. Clear the field, then use the brief transition to collect drops before the next camp.</p>

    <h2>3. Commit to a skill direction</h2>
    <p>Skill points are earned during the night, and the four branches support different approaches:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Blade:</strong> close-range pressure and direct damage.</li>
        <li><strong>Shadow:</strong> daggers, marks, and precise dash timing.</li>
        <li><strong>Flame:</strong> defensive tools that help protect the hunter and torch.</li>
        <li><strong>Curse:</strong> ranged spells and control over groups of enemies.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Choose skills that complement one another instead of spreading points across unrelated effects. A branch's Oath can be powerful, but it also comes with a cost.</p>

    <h2>4. Spend camp gold deliberately</h2>
    <p>At camp, gold can buy relics or help restore the torch. The torch does not recover for free between hours, so decide whether your current run needs more protection or another upgrade before spending.</p>

    <h2>5. Prepare for the boss hours</h2>
    <p>The main bosses arrive at hours 4, 8, and 12. Keep enough room to dodge, learn each attack pattern, and avoid getting pinned against the edge of the arena. The dash briefly protects the hunter, so timing it through an incoming attack is more useful than using it as soon as it is ready.</p>

    <h2>Controls</h2>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Computer:</strong> move with the mouse or WASD; use the left mouse button to throw daggers; press Space or Shift, or right-click, to dash.</li>
        <li><strong>Phone:</strong> play in landscape with the on-screen joystick and dagger and dash buttons.</li>
    </ul>
