    <h1>The map of Last Torch and its bosses</h1>
    <p>The night is a journey across six screens. A map follows your hunter, each wave can earn up to three stars, and every screen ends with a boss.</p>

    <h2>1. Dark Forest</h2>
    <p>Where every night begins. Skeletons and bats test your aim; learn to heal inside the torch's dashed circle.</p>
    <h2>2. River Crossing</h2>
    <p>Wet ground and new monsters. The <strong>River Spirit</strong> guards the crossing.</p>
    <h2>3. Swamp</h2>
    <p>Slow mud and toxic things. <strong>Slizork</strong> rules the bog.</p>
    <h2>4. Graveyard</h2>
    <p>The dead do not stay down. The <strong>Grave King</strong> waits among the tombs.</p>
    <h2>5. Village</h2>
    <p>Burning houses, armored soldiers. The <strong>Blood Countess</strong> hunts the survivors.</p>
    <h2>6. Castle</h2>
    <p>The last climb. Azael follows you all the way to his castle.</p>

    <p>Tip: spend gold at camp between waves and keep a skill for the boss. See the <a href="{{ '/en/guides/last-torch-survival-guide/' | relative_url }}">beginner's guide</a> and the <a href="{{ '/en/guides/last-torch-heroes/' | relative_url }}">hero list</a>.</p>
