    <h1>The heroes of Last Torch</h1>
    <p>Every hero changes how a night plays out. Pick the one that matches your style, then unlock the rest by finishing waves and spending embers in the Sanctuary.</p>

    <h2>Demon Hunter</h2>
    <p><img src="{{ '/assets/images/common/mini-game/last_torch/hero-hunter.webp' | relative_url }}" alt="Demon Hunter" width="320" loading="lazy"></p>
    <p>Patient and strategic. <strong>Steady Hand:</strong> attacks faster standing still, and the torch warms and heals him more. Free from the start.</p>

    <h2>Rowan</h2>
    <p><img src="{{ '/assets/images/common/mini-game/last_torch/hero-rowan.webp' | relative_url }}" alt="Rowan" width="320" loading="lazy"></p>
    <p>A druid who never fights alone. <strong>Forest Guardian:</strong> throws roses instead of daggers while the forest fights beside him. Unlock: survive Wave 1.</p>

    <h2>Father Avgustino</h2>
    <p><img src="{{ '/assets/images/common/mini-game/last_torch/hero-avgustino.webp' | relative_url }}" alt="Father Avgustino" width="320" loading="lazy"></p>
    <p>A priest of few words. <strong>Silent Vigil:</strong> the torch warms and heals him more, and silver bites harder. Unlock with embers.</p>

    <h2>Gudran</h2>
    <p><img src="{{ '/assets/images/common/mini-game/last_torch/hero-gudran.webp' | relative_url }}" alt="Gudran" width="320" loading="lazy"></p>
    <p>Lord of the Castle. <strong>Lord of the Castle:</strong> his staff strikes with real weight, and a click raises it to hurl a ball of blue energy. Unlock with embers.</p>

    <h2>Ninja Kuro</h2>
    <p>Nobody sees him coming. <strong>Silent Step:</strong> two dashes and fast shurikens, but fragile if caught. Unlock with embers.</p>

    <h2>Azael</h2>
    <p><img src="{{ '/assets/images/common/mini-game/last_torch/hero-azael.webp' | relative_url }}" alt="Azael" width="320" loading="lazy"></p>
    <p>A demon who hunts his own kind, up close. <strong>Blood Drinker:</strong> heals part of the damage he deals; hits hard, breaks easily. Unlock: beat the game.</p>

    <h2>Captain Brutos</h2>
    <p><img src="{{ '/assets/images/common/mini-game/last_torch/hero-brutos.webp' | relative_url }}" alt="Captain Brutos" width="320" loading="lazy"></p>
    <p>Slow and iron-clad. <strong>Heavy Hands:</strong> crushing axe blows and thick armor; instead of daggers he slams the ground. Unlock: defeat Captain Brutos.</p>
