This includes, where applicable:

- Characters and their origin stories;
- Names, terminology, and original worldbuilding elements;

- Original illustrations and artwork;

Rights to any third-party materials used remain with their respective owners or holders.

This list identifies project materials; it does not mean that every name, title, idea, or concept is independently protected by copyright. Protection depends on the type of material and applicable law; trademark rights may apply separately.

## Copyright {#copyright}

The original content available on this website is protected by applicable copyright laws and intellectual property regulations.

In many jurisdictions, copyright protection generally arises automatically when an original work is created and fixed in a tangible form. Registration is not generally required for protection, though its evidentiary value and effect on available remedies vary by jurisdiction.

This website makes the work available for personal, non-commercial reading. Public availability of the content **does not grant a general license for reproduction, distribution, or adaptation**.

Specifically, **no permission is granted** to:
- Republish chapters or the complete work on other websites (such as Wattpad, Amazon, blogs, or forums);
- Provide digital copies for external download or distribution;
- Translate and publish the work as an authorized version;
- Adapt the work into books, comics, games, videos, animations, or other formats;
- Use characters or original world elements in commercial projects;

Nothing on this page is intended to limit exceptions or limitations provided by applicable law, including quotation rights.

The creation of non-commercial fan content (such as fan art, non-monetized fan fiction, or cosplay) is welcomed and encouraged, provided proper credit is given to the original work.

Fan creations do not imply any transfer or grant of rights over the characters or universe. The author reserves the right to request the removal of content that violates these terms.

Translating a protected work involves derivative rights related to the original text.

Substantial or full translations of **"Yara, Demon Hunter"** intended for publication or distribution require prior authorization. Official versions available on this website or authorized platforms are the only recognized releases.
