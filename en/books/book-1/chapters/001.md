High on the mountain, far above the trees, someone was watching.

The creature stood motionless at the edge of the cliff, where the rock ended in a sheer drop down to the valley. Far below, among the shadows, small lights flickered, surrounded by a dense forest wrapped in fog. The village.

The wind blew strongly, making the dark cloak ripple behind its body. The torn edges rose into the air like deformed wings. Beneath the hood, very light strands of hair escaped and danced in front of its face. A dark band crossed its forehead, too tight to look like mere clothing.

Its breath formed no vapor in the freezing air.

It did not waver before the height.

Its eyes remained fixed on the distant lights, as if waiting for the exact moment.

For a long instant, nothing moved.

Then, slowly, one hand rose to its face.

Long fingers, ending in dark, hard nails like small claws, touched the bandage.

Its entire body contracted.

For a brief instant, a memory flashed across its expression: the reflection of a blade, a flash of light, an ancient pain.

The hand gripped the fabric.

Then, it relaxed.

— So... — The voice came out low and hoarse, heavy with the weight of years. — This is it.

The wind carried the words into the void.

It stepped forward.

Its foot met the rock with silent firmness. The compact toes adjusted to the stone, gripping with a naturalness that did not belong to a man.

The creature tilted its head.

It listened.

The wind.

The trees.

The distant sounds of the night.

Then, for a moment, the shadow of the hood retreated.

Pale skin, of a nearly imperceptible slight bluish tone, emerged among the shadows. Ancient scars cut across its face. Its lips curved into a brief smile, revealing long fangs before they vanished back beneath the hood.

It had noticed something.

— I felt you... — it murmured.

Its eyes narrowed.

Silence.

*But you can also feel me; I need to be cautious.*

The bandages wrapping one of its arms tightened.

The creature looked at one of its own hands.

For a few seconds, it remained motionless.

Then it extended its fingers.

A very faint reddish energy appeared between them.

It was almost imperceptible.

The creature frowned.

It tried to concentrate it.

The energy trembled.

For an instant, it seemed to gain strength.

Then it dissolved.

Small reddish fragments disappeared into the air.

The creature kept looking at its own hand.

It tried again.

Nothing.

Its expression hardened.

The fingers closed slowly.

— ...

It lowered its hand.

There was no understanding on its face.

Only frustration.

— Still here.

A pause.

— After so long...

— I've waited too long to lose my temper now. You hid well. Very well.

— But time has passed for you. You're weaker... unlike me.

Its hand closed tightly.

A thick, dark liquid trickled between its fingers.

— This time, you won't escape.

The creature stepped back from the edge. The cloak enveloped its silhouette completely once again.

Its steps were firm.

Silent.

And, little by little, its figure disappeared among the shadows.

Down below, the village remained asleep.

No one knew it had been there.

And no one could imagine that, on that night, the creature had finally found what it was looking for.

The threat did not come from the forest.

It had already found its way to them.
