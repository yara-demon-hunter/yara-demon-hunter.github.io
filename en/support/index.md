<h2 id="why-support">1. Why support the project?</h2>

This project is developed as a long-form universe that includes books, characters, lore, games, and digital experiences. Support helps keep the story alive, improve the website, expand the world, and invest in future content and interactive work.

<h2 id="support-options">2. Support options</h2>

The project currently offers support through Ko-fi, and community support through direct feedback and engagement.

You can support the project by:

<ul>
    <li>donating through Ko-fi;</li>
    <li>sharing the story and game pages with other readers;</li>
    <li>sending feedback and ideas;</li>
    <li>following the project updates and helping spread the word.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="ko-fi">3. Ko-fi</h2>

Support the project directly through Ko-fi, where you can make a contribution and help cover the work behind the story, the website, and the games.

<h2 id="community">4. Community support</h2>

Community support matters as much as donations. Sharing the story, discussing the world, and helping others discover the project all contribute to its growth as a living universe.
