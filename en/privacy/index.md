# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 22, 2026**

Yara, Demon Hunter ("we", "us", or "this website") respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website and how that information is used.

<h2 id="information-we-collect">1. Information We Collect</h2>

This website does not require visitors to create an account or provide personal information in order to read the story.

However, third-party services used by this website may collect information about visitors, including technical information such as:

* IP address;
* browser and device information;
* pages visited;
* approximate geographic information;
* referral information;
* interactions with the website.

The information collected depends on the services being used and their respective privacy policies.

<h2 id="google-analytics">2. Google Analytics</h2>

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website.

Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device information, and interactions with the website.

This information is used to understand website traffic and improve the website and its content.

Google may use cookies and similar technologies to collect this information.

For more information, please see Google's privacy documentation:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

This website may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure advertisements, depending on the user's settings and applicable requirements.

For more information about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can also learn more about Google's advertising policies and controls through Google's advertising settings.

This website may provide links or support options through Ko-fi.

If you choose to interact with Ko-fi, your interaction is subject to Ko-fi's own privacy policy and terms.

This website does not control the information collected by Ko-fi.

This website and third-party services used by the website may use cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies may be used for purposes such as analytics, advertising, website functionality, and remembering preferences.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features or third-party services may not function correctly if cookies are disabled.

This website may contain links to or integrations with third-party services.

These services operate independently and have their own privacy policies and terms.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services.

<h2 id="childrens-privacy">7. Children's Privacy</h2>

This website is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
