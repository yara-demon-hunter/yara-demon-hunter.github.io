# Notas da atualização para a CrazyGames (17/11/2026)

Texto pronto para colar, em inglês. Cobre o que mudou desde o relatório de 16/11. Não menciona ferramentas de desenvolvimento.

```
Update notes

NEW
- Embers & Altar: every night now earns Embers, win or lose. Spend them at the new Altar
  (start screen and end-of-night screen) on 10 permanent upgrades: health, damage, magic damage,
  move speed, dagger range, Fury power, dash distance, an extra dash charge, starting skill
  points and torch health. Your progress carries over to the next night.
- Starting skill points are now shown next to Darkness on the start screen.

BETTER FIRST MINUTES
- Much faster loading, especially on slow connections.
- In Wave 1, a hand now points to the skill medal ("Upgrade your skills"), and the
  "Stay near the torch to heal" tip shows every run.
- Shorter first duel on your first night.

MOBILE & SCREEN SIZES
- HUD and touch controls (joystick, dagger, dash) now adapt to every screen size and
  orientation: portrait, landscape, tablets and desktop.
- Skill tree fits portrait screens without scrolling the page; long skill descriptions
  scroll inside their panel.
- Boss health bar moved to its own row under the hero card in portrait.
- Start screen: smaller title, and the Sanctuary/Altar buttons and locked-hero hints
  no longer overflow.

FIXES
- Hero card no longer stretches on narrow landscape phones.
- Light Path tab no longer spills out of the skill tree frame on small screens.
```
